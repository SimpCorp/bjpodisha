Status rules, no drama.

Green · Fulfilled: The promise has clear public evidence of implementation or completion.
Orange · Work in progress: The promise has started, but the full target or final outcome is not complete yet.
Red · Deadline passed: A clear deadline was promised and available evidence does not show completion.
Evidence standard: Prefer official government/party sources first, then reliable news reports for verification.
